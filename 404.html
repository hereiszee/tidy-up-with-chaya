Meet Chaya, Founder of Tidy Up With Chaya

I believe an organized home should feel good to live in, not just look good.

My approach is about making the most of what you already have, finding creative ways to reuse and repurpose items, and thoughtfully sourcing solutions when needed. Inspired by the KonMari Method, I guide clients through decluttering, deciding what to keep, and finding new homes for items through donations and giveaways.

Together, we create functional, personalized spaces that bring more ease, calm, and purpose to everyday living.
